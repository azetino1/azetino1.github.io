Half-Day Mountain Bike Tours

$140 per person or $120 per person for two or more people

Who: This ride is aimed at beginner to intermediate riders.

Where: We will ride on gravel USFS roads and some double track with gradual inclines and descents.

How Long: Allow four hours for this adventure. Plan on biking for approximately two hours.

What Is Provided: A full-suspension mountain bike, helmet, guide, and transportation from Canyon Adventures.

What You Need to Bring: Water, a backpack—tell us ahead of time if you need to borrow one—an energy bar, an extra layer, a rain jacket, sunglasses, sunscreen, and sturdy shoes. No sandals.
