Full-Day Mountain Bike Tours

$220 per person or $180 per person for two or more people

Who: Intermediate to advanced intermediate.

Where: USFS gravel roads, double track, and possibly single track, depending on ability.

How Long: Give yourself the greater part of the day for this ride. Actual riding time is usually between three and four hours.

What Is Provided: Full-suspension mountain bike, helmet, guide, sack lunch if requested, and transportation from Canyon Adventures.

What You Need to Bring: Water, a backpack—tell us ahead of time if you need to borrow one—energy bars, an extra layer, a rain jacket, sunglasses, sunscreen, and sturdy shoes. No sandals.
