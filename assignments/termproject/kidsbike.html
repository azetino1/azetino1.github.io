XTC JR 1 24 Disc 2018

Price: $400

Size: One size

Colors: Electric Blue/Neon Green/White

Tires: Giant Junior Lite Sport, 24x1.95

Brakes: Giant DA6 mechanical disc, 140mm

Big-time off-road features in a mini package. With front suspension and disc brakes, young riders are ready for the trail. The disc-brake version of the XTC Jr. provides additional control for more advanced trails and conditions.

Its features include a lightweight ALUXX-grade butted aluminum frame, a 50mm-travel suspension fork with lockout, Shimano 3x7-speed shifting, Giant mechanical disc brakes, 24-inch alloy rims, alloy hubs, stainless-steel spokes, a bell, and a kickstand.
